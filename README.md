Entry for the make.fun competition [Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf).

## Why this line

Candidates: (A) the chosen large-language-model line, (B) a neural net that read ten thousand romance novels and understood none, (C) a partner who drafted the wedding vows in twelve tones. A won against the judge brief: it reads as a dating bio with a character's wants and habits, the laughs come from specific absurdities of being an AI (total recall, confident invention, instant replies), the punch lands at the end, and it avoids the worn robot-in-search-of-love angle.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) validates the fixed entry line against the competition gate and writes it to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), then reads the file back to verify the bytes.
